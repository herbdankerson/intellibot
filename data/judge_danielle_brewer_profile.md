# Judge Danielle Brewer Background

The Honorable Danielle Brewer currently serves on Florida's Twelfth Judicial Circuit
Court. Governor Ron DeSantis elevated her to the circuit bench on July 4, 2022 after
she had served as a DeSoto County Court judge since her 2016 appointment by Governor
Rick Scott. Judge Brewer's professional background includes four years as an
associate attorney at Swaine & Harris, P.A. prior to joining the judiciary. She is an
alumna of the University of Florida (B.S.) and Mercer University's Walter F. George
School of Law (J.D.). Brewer resides in Arcadia and continues to preside over matters
affecting Sarasota and DeSoto counties.
